Reference: A Benchmark for the Evaluation of RGB-D SLAM Systems, IROS 2012.

This fixture extracts one RGB frame and its nearest depth frame from the sequence.
The original PNG pixels are retained. Derived assets include a Float32 depth
buffer, an 8-bit depth visualization, and person masks, cutouts and joints
computed locally with Apple Vision. No endorsement by the authors is implied.
The sensor is a Kinect; this is not an iPhone LiDAR photo.
